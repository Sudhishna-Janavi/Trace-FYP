Why aggregation is the hard part

No two banks export the same file. One writes a single signed amount, another splits debits and credits into separate columns, a third issues a fresh file every month and records purchases as positive. Each is read by its own adapter and normalised into one ledger.

Every adapter also discards the bank's own category column. Three banks disagree about what a category is and none of them agrees with the others across institutions, so a category that holds across all of a person's accounts has to be worked out from the description. That is the reason the classifier exists.
